FamBoard Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · App: FamBoard (Android, package com.famboard.app) · Developer: Sapps

In short: FamBoard has no accounts, no servers of ours, no ads and no tracking. What your family adds stays on your family's phones. Phones in the same family sync directly with each other over Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth, encrypted with a key that only those phones hold. We, the developer, never receive your data.

1. What FamBoard stores

FamBoard stores what you and your family add to the app: items to buy, repairs, bills (names, amounts, due dates), tasks, saved lists, the names you give family members and phones, and your settings. This is stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database. The encryption key is kept in your phone's secure key store.

2. How family sync works

When phones in the same family are near each other (same Wi‑Fi network, or in Bluetooth/Wi‑Fi Direct range using Google Play services Nearby Connections), FamBoard exchanges changes directly between those phones. Every connection is encrypted end to end (X25519 key exchange and ChaCha20‑Poly1305) and both phones must prove they hold the family's secret key, which is shared only through the family's invite QR code. The data does not pass through any server, and we cannot read it.

3. Data we collect

We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. FamBoard has no analytics or advertising SDKs and does not use the advertising ID.

Some features use Google components that run on your phone. Google may receive limited technical information from them, under Google's Privacy Policy:

FeatureWhat happens
Bill scanning (Google ML Kit text recognition and document scanner)Bill photos are read on your phone; FamBoard does not upload them. ML Kit may send Google diagnostic information such as device model, OS and app version, API performance and error codes.
Voice entryWhen you tap the microphone, your phone's speech recognition service turns speech into text. Depending on your phone and language, that service (usually Google) may process the audio online. FamBoard only receives the resulting text and does not store audio.
Nearby syncUses Google Play services Nearby Connections to find family phones nearby. Your FamBoard data is encrypted before it is sent.

FamBoard does not read your SMS. To add a bill from an SMS, you copy or share the message into the app yourself.

5. Sharing and exports you choose

When you share a list or bill (for example to WhatsApp, as a PDF or CSV), create a backup file, or send an invite, the content goes to the app or person you choose. Backups are encrypted with a passphrase you set; we never see it.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until you or your family delete it. You can delete any item in the app, and a family admin can remove members. Uninstalling FamBoard deletes everything on that phone. Copies on other family members' phones stay there until they delete them or uninstall. Since we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; if you have questions, contact us below.

7. Children

FamBoard is meant for households and is used by adults and teenagers in a family. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Security

Data is encrypted on the phone and in transit between family phones. No method is perfect; keep your phone locked and share invite QR codes only with family.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
