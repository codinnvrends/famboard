FamBoard is one shared board for everything your home needs: groceries that ran out, a tap that's leaking, the electricity bill that's due, the fridge you've been meaning to upgrade. Everyone in the family adds to it, everyone sees it, and nothing gets forgotten.

No internet? No problem. FamBoard syncs directly between your family's phones over Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth. There is no account to create and no server in the middle.

★ ONE BOARD FOR THE WHOLE HOME
• Add out-of-stock items, things to buy, repairs, upgrades, bills and chores
• Today view shows what's overdue, urgent and coming up this week
• Mark who is handling what ("Raj is on it") and tick things off when done
• Propose an upgrade and let the family weigh in

★ TYPE IT OR SAY IT
• Quick add with voice entry
• Automatic categories: "bathroom tap leaking" becomes a plumbing repair, "doodh khatam" goes to Dairy
• Understands English, Hindi and Hinglish

★ SHOPPING MADE SIMPLE
• One shopping list for everyone, grouped by aisle
• Tick items off as you shop; the rest of the family sees it

★ NEVER MISS A BILL
• Monthly, quarterly, yearly and one-time bills with due-date reminders
• See what's still to pay and what's been paid this month
• Paste or share a bill SMS and FamBoard fills in the amount and due date

★ SCAN A BILL, KEEP THE LIST
• Photograph a shop bill and FamBoard reads the items on your phone
• Fix any line, then save it as a list for next month
• Share lists on WhatsApp, or as a PDF or spreadsheet

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your family's data stays on your family's phones
• Phone-to-phone sync is end-to-end encrypted
• No ads, no tracking, no sign-up
• Encrypted backups you control

★ MADE FOR INDIAN HOMES
• English and Hindi, with more languages coming
• Works on Android 8 and newer

Invite family members with a QR code and you're set. FamBoard is free.
